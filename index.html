I'm an independent contributor (IC) with the Data & AI Science team at Intuit Credit Karma, where I focus on improving financial product and action recommendation systems.

Outside of my work there, I'm an independent researcher in sensor fusion and tracking. My research interests sit at the cross section between machine learning and estimation theory, with a focus on making estimation and tracking algorithms more explainable. I'm also interested in leveraging reinforcement learning for autonomous mobile agents in hazardous and unknown environments.

I received my PhD in 2009 from the University of Connecticut under Yaakov Bar-Shalom, specializing in multi-sensor data fusion and target tracking. I joined Intuit through its acquisition of Zendrive's technology, where I had been a Principal Data Scientist.

See my publications, browse selected projects, or read my technical writing. For collaboration or questions, please get in touch.
